Malabe - Panadura

The shuttle departs from Malabe Campus at 8:00 AM and arrives in Panadura at approximately 8:45 AM. The route takes the A1 Highway, making stops at Talawathugoda, Pannipitiya, and Hokandara along the way.

Malabe - Negombo

Shuttle service from Malabe leaves at 9:00 AM and reaches Negombo by 10:00 AM. This route utilizes the A3 Road, stopping at Maharagama, Kaduwela, and Kelaniya before arriving at the destination.

Malabe - Colombo

Departing from Malabe Campus at 7:30 AM, the shuttle arrives in Colombo by 8:15 AM. The route follows Nawala Road with stops at Nugegoda, Maharagama, and Borella.

Malabe - Kottawa

Shuttles depart from Malabe at 7:45 AM and reach Kottawa at 8:15 AM. The route takes Pannipitiya Road, with stops at Talawathugoda and Pannipitiya.

Malabe - Wattala

Departing from Malabe at 9:00 AM, the shuttle arrives in Wattala by 9:30 AM. The shuttle travels via A1 Highway, with stops at Peliyagoda and Kelaniya.

Malabe - Wellawatte

The shuttle leaves Malabe at 8:15 AM and reaches Wellawatte by 8:45 AM. The route follows Galle Road, making stops at Mount Lavinia and Ratmalana.

Notices :-

  • QR Code for Boarding: Each passenger must present a valid QR code to board the shuttle. QR codes will be provided upon successful payment.
